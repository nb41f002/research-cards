05 · Prompt Cue Cards

Prompt Cue Cards

The front is an image.
The back is language.

Five elements — subject, action, scene, style, and atmosphere — guided a cycle of select → combine → enter → compare → revise. The scaffold did not think for students; it prepared a path for thinking.

The first cycle used Dreamina for six lessons. Reflection on scaffolding, differentiation, and campus information security informed later planning with Gemini 2.5 Flash Image.

07 · Finding I

Finding I

From one noun to a complete visual sentence.

Early

“Cat” — a single noun.

Middle

“A bird flying in the sky, autumn” — scaffold-supported structure.

Later

“Comic-style illustration, a small golden butterfly” — independently constructed.

Around 60% of students reached compound five-element narratives by lessons 3–4. The cue cards also helped an SEN student cross the expression barrier, strengthening transferable language-organization skills.

08 · Finding II

Finding II

Immediate feedback became an unexpected push.

<10Minutes of attention before the course
20–30Minutes of sustained engagement
3×One student generated three works from the same prompt
Short loopPrompt → image → curiosity → revision

The combination of immediate feedback and manageable challenge approximated conditions associated with flow, while AI’s stochastic outputs also sustained curiosity.

09 · Teacher Support

Teacher Support

What did the teacher say when frustration arrived?

Normalize

“I also adjusted mine several times before it worked.”

Reframe

“Why do you think the AI drew it this way?” Failure becomes evidence.

Micro-success

Temporarily lower difficulty, rebuild efficacy, then return to the original challenge.

These strategies were constructed responsively in the classroom and supported students across a wide emotional spectrum, from sighs and jokes to anger and withdrawal.

10 · Transfer

Transfer

The course ended.
The capability did not.

Individual continuation

One student returned weekly to continue creating at the challenge level.

Self-directed use

Students created during free time without an assignment.

Cross-course transfer

The five-element structure reappeared in AI music class through self-designed album covers.

11 · Professional Transformation

From knowledge transmitter to a regulator of learning emotions and a guide for thinking scaffolds.

The central question is not whether a stronger AI tool exists, but whether a teacher is willing to notice frustration and guide thought.
