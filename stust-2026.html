Does a Learning Gap Remain under Generative-AI Support? A Quantitative Educational-Equity Analysis across Junior-High Technology Courses

Graduate student: Yeh, Jung-Chen · Advisor: Li, Chia-Yi
Southern Taiwan University of Science and Technology · 2026 International Conference on Interdisciplinary AI and Digital Humanities · October 2, 2026
02 · The Premise

From Ideas to Artifacts

With access to AI,
what support do students need?

Some students struggle to type; others can type but need help describing their ideas. The researcher calls this the Conceptual Materialization Dilemma.

Typing practice supports text entry. Formulas, picture cards and a music-prompt website support expression. The study examines participation records and group outcomes within this teacher-designed course.

05 · Intervention

Intervention

Support across two semesters.

Semester I · AI Images

A prompt formula and five-element cue cards turned unclear ideas into structured language.

Semester II · SUNO Music

A three-tier scaffold: theme frames, a prompt-building website, and interactive revision.

Same students

Six lessons per unit; each task is graded by its own course rubric. Both units address the “Conceptual Materialization Dilemma” named by the researcher: turning a story a student wants to tell into a precise prompt.

09 · The Number That Matters

Scores of exactly 60

25.8%

of SEN students scored exactly 60,

A score of exactly 60 means a song was submitted with the prompt left blank or copied from the template. Prompts are where SEN students needed more support.

Valid SUNO scores: 487 students, SEN 8/31 and general 53/456. This descriptive sample is distinct from the 486-student ANCOVA model.

11 · Scope

Study Context

Understand learning
within a supported course.

One public junior high school, 18 classes and two semesters. The researcher independently designed and taught the course; a special-education assistant supported individual students.

Drawing and music used task-specific grading. Q1 describes an input indicator, Q2 traces platform records, and Q3 compares group outcomes.

Separating AI and teacher-scaffolding effects requires comparison conditions. Ceiling effects in drawing scores, 31 SEN model cases and the class structure also inform interpretation.

12 · The Heart

Support and Outcomes

Support text entry.
Support expression.
Keep examining the gap.

The typing indicator increased by 2.76 characters/minute, traceable work records increased, and the adjusted group difference was about 3 points.

Student work illustrates how support was used. The next question is where students still encounter barriers and how to support more independent creation.
