Reshaping the Teacher’s Role in an Age of AI Empowerment: Emotional Leadership and Scaffolding in Generative-AI-Integrated Junior-High Instruction

Yeh, Jung-Chen · Southern Taiwan University of Science and Technology
“From Local Contexts to Global Networks: Reimagining Teacher Education” · National Tsing Hua University · Poster 44 (C09) · May 23, 2026
02 · The Moment

The Moment

Generation took ten seconds.
Frustration arrived immediately after.

“Teacher, this is not what I wanted.” “Why is it still wrong?” “I can’t do it.” The classroom revealed that teaching with AI was no longer only about operating technology.

03 · The Real Gap

The Real Gap

The challenge is the art of guiding human–AI collaboration.

Cognitive scaffolding

How can teachers help students cross linguistic and conceptual barriers in generation?

Emotional leadership

How can teacher responses transform anxiety and frustration into another attempt?

Professional role

What substantive changes occur in teachers’ work during AI-supported co-creation?

04 · Methodology

Methodology

Treat AI as a co-creative partner, not merely a tool.

A six-week action-research implementation combined the ADDIE instructional-design model with four theoretical pillars.

ADDIE

Systematic planning, implementation, evaluation, and iterative revision.

Scaffolding

Temporary support between current ability and the learning goal.

Cognitive load

Reduce extraneous demands so attention can remain on core creative choices.

Flow

Balance challenge and skill to support intrinsic motivation.

05 · Semester I · Visual

Semester I · Visual

Turn abstract imagery into actionable prompts.

AI image creation was paired with self-designed visual prompt cards. Teachers gradually moved from whole-class demonstration toward individualized guidance as students learned to decompose an imagined scene into prompt elements.

Visual cue cardsPrompt scaffoldingCross-disciplinary narrativeConcept visualization
06 · Semester II · Sonic

Semester II · Sonic

From listeners to creators.

SUNO and a custom Chinese-lyrics GPT were combined with a dropdown scaffold that lowered linguistic barriers. Students iterated from lyric ideas to musical style, while teachers supported both revision and emotional regulation.

Dropdown scaffoldSUNO co-creationMultilingual compositionEmotional regulation
07 · Finding I

Finding I

When the entry barrier falls, students can spend attention on creativity.

Visual cards and dropdown menus acted as cognitive mediators. Students no longer had to handle language, concepts, and technical operations all at once.

Lower extraneous load

The scaffold externalized choices and sequence.

Differentiated support

Visual or structured pathways matched different learning needs.

ZPD in AI learning

Support was gradually withdrawn as students gained control.

08 · Finding II

Finding II

One empathic sentence can turn anxiety into momentum.

Teacher empathy and reframing helped students move from generation failure toward renewed attempts and flow.

The study positions emotional leadership as a central instructional intervention, not an optional interpersonal extra.

09 · Threefold Transformation

Threefold Transformation

The teacher’s role is being reshaped.

Cognitive Scaffolder

Builds decomposable and actionable support between ability and goal.

Ethical Arbiter

Clarifies the boundaries among creation, attribution, borrowing, and plagiarism.

Emotional Leader

Stabilizes frustration and converts anxiety into energy for another attempt.

10 · Beyond Technology

Whatever technology changes, education must return to the empowerment and care of people. AI should carry human warmth rather than remain a cold tool.
